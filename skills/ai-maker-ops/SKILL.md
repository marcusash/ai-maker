# AI Maker Ops

Communication, sprint operations, and team coordination for managers. This skill helps you run effective sprints, communicate clearly with stakeholders, and keep projects moving — without drowning in administrative overhead.

## When to invoke

Use this skill when you need to:
- Draft a status update, stakeholder email, or escalation
- Plan or run a sprint: backlog grooming, sprint planning, retrospective
- Write a meeting agenda, decision log, or action item summary
- Coordinate across teams or unblock a dependency
- Translate between engineering and business language

## What it does

1. **Communication drafting** — writes emails, Slack messages, status updates, and escalations calibrated to the audience (engineer vs executive vs customer)
2. **Sprint operations** — structures sprint planning, backlog grooming, and retrospectives with clear inputs and outputs
3. **Meeting facilitation** — writes agendas, captures decisions, generates action items with owners and due dates
4. **Coordination** — identifies blockers, suggests resolution paths, drafts dependency requests
5. **Translation** — converts engineering status into business impact language and vice versa

## Key behaviors

- Audience-aware — tone and detail level shift based on who receives the communication
- Action-oriented — every output has a clear next step or decision
- Concise — no filler; respects the reader's time
- Escalation-calibrated — knows when something is a heads-up vs requires a decision vs is a crisis

## Output formats

- Email or Slack message draft
- Sprint planning agenda + story point guidance
- Retrospective format with action items
- Meeting notes with decisions and owners
- Stakeholder status update (1-pager format)

## Scope

This skill covers management communication and sprint operations. For M365 integration (sending email, scheduling meetings, Teams), use **AI Maker WorkIQ**.
