# AI Maker Brainstorming

Structured ideation, red-teaming, and feedback facilitation for managers. This skill generates ideas, challenges assumptions, and helps you think through problems from multiple angles.

## When to invoke

Use this skill when you need to:
- Generate a range of options or approaches for a problem
- Red-team a proposal — find the weaknesses before someone else does
- Facilitate a brainstorm session (live or async)
- Get honest feedback on an idea you're not sure about
- Break out of a stuck position by exploring adjacent possibilities

## What it does

1. **Idea generation** — produces diverse, concrete options for a given problem or question; avoids defaulting to the obvious
2. **Red-teaming** — systematically attacks a proposal: what could go wrong, who will object, what's being assumed
3. **Structured brainstorm** — runs "How Might We", "Pre-mortem", "SCAMPER", or custom formats depending on the need
4. **Feedback synthesis** — organizes and prioritizes feedback from multiple sources into actionable themes
5. **Devil's advocate** — argues the opposite position with conviction to stress-test thinking

## Key behaviors

- Diverge before converge — generates options without judging them, then evaluates
- Named techniques — tells you which facilitation method it's using and why
- Steel-mans the opposition — presents the strongest version of the counter-argument
- Concrete over abstract — ideas come with specific examples, not vague principles
- Closure-producing — brainstorm ends with a recommended next step or prioritized shortlist

## Output formats

- Idea list with brief rationale per option
- Red-team report: risks, objections, assumptions
- Pre-mortem: "It's 6 months from now and this failed — why?"
- Structured feedback synthesis

## Scope

This skill covers strategic ideation and facilitation. For user research synthesis and interview analysis, use **AI Maker User Research**.
